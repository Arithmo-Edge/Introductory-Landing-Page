01 · Strict No-Mixed ELO Policy · 10 Core Arena ELOs

5 Core Arenas & 60 Canonical ELO Titles

Arithmo-Edge prohibits single-bucket rating inflation through its Strict No-Mixed ELO Policy: every competitor maintains 10 isolated Core Arena ELOs (an independent General ELO and Expert ELO inside each of the 5 arenas), ensuring fast arithmetic solvers cannot coast to proof-level Grandmaster titles without proven theorem mastery.

04 · Zero-Fragmentation Olympiad Tag Policy

Tags Locked in Rated Rounds · Unlocked in Practice

In authentic Olympiads like the IMO or Putnam, problems never arrive pre-labeled with the theorem required to solve them. Sub-topics such as #Modular-Arithmetic, #Vieta-Jumping, #Inversion, and #Pigeonhole are strictly hidden during live rated contests and unlocked exclusively during unrated practice and `Arithmo Database` review.

Live Rated Mode
Topic Hints Stripped

Only Arena domain and Segment difficulty appear; TagArchitect suppresses sub-topic strings client & edge side.

Unrated Practice & Database
Granular Theorem Search

Full tag filtering unlocks post-contest so WeaknessTargetedGen AI can target specific lemma gaps.
