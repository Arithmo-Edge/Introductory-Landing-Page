Replacing Static Gatekept Forums with Live ELO Proof Warfare

Traditional Olympiad mathematics platforms leave learners in fragmented static PDFs, unrated forum threads, or opaque manual grading queues. Arithmo-Edge transforms Olympiad mathematics into a live, sport-grade discipline with deterministic client-side verification, strict topic-tag hiding during rated hours, and real-time ELO telemetry.

Every architectural decision enforces Zero Pay-to-Win: subscriptions and Arithmo Wallet credits expand AI mentoring throughput and Forge capacity, while every single ELO point is earned purely through verified mathematical solutions and post-contest proof auditing.

Competitors without a registered @handle can freely browse all 14 public leaderboards on Subsite #7, practice past archives, and submit answers inside live contests as Unrated (0 ELO change).

Claiming a unique @handle via Gmail SSO unlocks rated ELO tracking across all 10 Core Arena ELOs, 1v1 Bullet Blitz, closed-loop Arithmo Wallet gifting, Academy Dual-Handle routing, Arithmo Forge, Socials, and Headquarters Core.
